PrimeShield Vehicle Protection Agreement

This agreement describes the general coverage terms associated with a PrimeShield vehicle protection plan. Customers should review the applicable plan terms before purchasing coverage.

Covered Components

Subject to the selected plan and applicable terms, coverage may include failures of covered vehicle components caused by qualifying mechanical or electrical defects.

  • Engine components
  • Transmission components
  • Factory-installed electrical components

Exclusions

Coverage does not apply to conditions or damage resulting from accidents, misuse, neglect, or normal wear and tear, subject to the applicable terms of the selected plan.

Claims

All claims are subject to review and approval. Customers may be required to provide information about the vehicle, the issue being reported, and any applicable repair documentation.

Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for maintaining their vehicle according to the manufacturer's recommended maintenance requirements and for providing accurate information when purchasing coverage or submitting a claim.

Customer Agreement

By purchasing a PrimeShield plan, the customer acknowledges that they have reviewed and agreed to the applicable coverage terms, conditions, limitations, and exclusions.
