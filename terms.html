Coverage Terms & Conditions

Please review the terms before purchasing a PrimeShield plan.

1. Vehicle Protection Plan

PrimeShield provides vehicle protection plans designed to help customers with unexpected repair expenses for covered vehicle components, subject to the applicable plan terms, exclusions, limitations, and claim approval requirements.

A PrimeShield plan is a vehicle protection/service contract and is not traditional automobile insurance.

2. 30-Day Waiting Period

A 30-day waiting period applies after enrollment before eligible claims may be submitted for covered repairs.

Repairs or mechanical problems that occur before the waiting period has been completed are not eligible for coverage.

3. Active Subscription Required

Customers must maintain an active PrimeShield subscription and remain current with required payments in order to remain eligible for covered benefits.

A plan may be suspended or terminated if required payments are not maintained.

4. Pre-Existing Conditions

Pre-existing mechanical or electrical conditions are not covered.

A condition may be considered pre-existing when the failure or related symptoms existed before coverage became effective or before the applicable waiting period was completed.

5. Covered Repairs

Coverage applies only to eligible failures of components specifically covered under the customer's selected PrimeShield plan.

  • Engine components may be covered according to the selected plan.
  • Transmission components may be covered according to the selected plan.
  • Factory-installed electrical components may be covered according to the selected plan.

Coverage is subject to the applicable contract terms, exclusions, claim review, and authorization requirements.

6. Exclusions

PrimeShield does not provide coverage for every type of vehicle damage or repair. Unless specifically stated otherwise in the applicable plan, exclusions include:

  • Accidents or collision damage.
  • Misuse or abuse of the vehicle.
  • Neglect or failure to properly maintain the vehicle.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Pre-existing conditions.
  • Repairs or failures that do not meet the requirements of the applicable plan.

7. Claims Approval

All claims are subject to review and approval before covered repairs are authorized.

Submitting a claim does not automatically guarantee approval or payment. PrimeShield may request documentation, diagnostic information, maintenance records, or other information necessary to evaluate the claim.

8. Claim Authorization

Customers should not assume that a repair is covered or begin a covered repair without receiving the required authorization.

Unauthorized repairs may not qualify for reimbursement or payment under the applicable plan.

9. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information and properly maintaining their vehicle.

  • Provide accurate vehicle and customer information.
  • Follow the vehicle manufacturer's recommended maintenance requirements.
  • Provide requested documentation when submitting a claim.
  • Notify PrimeShield of covered mechanical problems through the applicable claims process.
  • Follow claim authorization instructions before beginning repairs.

10. Repair Facilities

Covered repairs must be performed at a repair facility that meets the requirements of the applicable PrimeShield plan and claim authorization process.

Customers may be required to obtain authorization before repair work begins.

11. Plan Duration & Automatic Renewal

PrimeShield plans operate according to the subscription period selected by the customer.

Monthly plans may automatically renew according to the customer's selected billing arrangement unless canceled in accordance with the applicable cancellation terms.

12. Cancellation & Refunds

Customers may request cancellation of their subscription according to the applicable cancellation procedure.

Any refund or cancellation adjustment will be handled according to the terms applicable to the customer's plan and payment arrangement.

13. Misuse & Fraud

PrimeShield reserves the right to suspend or terminate a plan when there is evidence of fraud, intentional misuse, false information, or other activity intended to improperly obtain benefits.

14. Accuracy of Customer Information

Customers are responsible for ensuring that all information submitted during registration, payment, and claims processing is accurate and complete.

Incorrect or misleading information may affect eligibility or claim approval.

15. No Guarantee of Claim Approval

Enrollment in a PrimeShield plan does not guarantee that every repair or mechanical problem will be covered.

Coverage depends on the customer's selected plan, the applicable contract terms, exclusions, eligibility requirements, and claim authorization.

16. Plan Pricing

PrimeShield currently offers the following plan options:

  • Basic: $299
  • Standard: $499
  • Premium: $799

Pricing and available plans may be subject to change. The price presented to the customer at the time of purchase will apply to that transaction.

17. Changes to Plans or Terms

PrimeShield may update its plans, services, website information, or terms when necessary.

Changes applicable to an existing customer's plan will be handled according to the applicable contract and legal requirements.

18. Applicable Contract

The customer's applicable vehicle protection contract and plan documents govern coverage, eligibility, exclusions, claims, cancellation, and other contractual obligations.

These website terms are intended to provide general information and should be reviewed together with the applicable customer contract.

19. Important Coverage Notice

PrimeShield vehicle protection plans are subject to eligibility requirements, waiting periods, exclusions, limitations, claim review, and authorization.

Customers should carefully review the applicable plan documents before purchasing coverage.

Coverage availability and contractual requirements may vary depending on the customer's location and applicable law.
