Coverage Terms & Conditions

Please review the terms before purchasing a PrimeShield plan.

1. Vehicle Protection Plan

PrimeShield provides vehicle protection plans designed to help customers with eligible covered repair expenses in accordance with the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations of the customer's selected plan.

2. 30-Day Waiting Period

Coverage begins 30 days after the initial payment, subject to the applicable terms of the selected plan. Repairs or mechanical problems occurring before coverage becomes effective are not eligible for coverage under the plan.

3. Active Subscription Required

The customer's plan must remain active and paid in order to maintain coverage. If a payment is not received when due, coverage may be suspended or terminated in accordance with the applicable cancellation and payment terms.

4. Pre-Existing Conditions

Existing vehicle issues, mechanical problems, damage, or conditions that occurred or existed before coverage became effective are not covered.

5. Covered Repairs

Coverage is limited to the components, systems, and repair services specifically identified as covered under the customer's selected PrimeShield plan. A component or repair is not covered simply because it is related to a covered vehicle system.

Customers should review the specific plan description provided at purchase for the applicable covered components and benefits.

6. Exclusions

Unless specifically stated otherwise in the customer's selected plan, PrimeShield does not cover repairs or damage resulting from excluded conditions or events.

  • Accidents or collision damage
  • Misuse, abuse, or improper operation of the vehicle
  • Neglect or failure to properly maintain the vehicle
  • Pre-existing mechanical or electrical conditions
  • Normal wear and tear where excluded by the selected plan
  • Fraudulent claims or intentionally caused damage

Additional exclusions or limitations may apply depending on the selected plan and the applicable contract terms.

7. Claims Approval

All claims are subject to review and approval based on the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations of the customer's selected plan. A customer should not assume that a repair is covered until PrimeShield has reviewed and authorized the claim.

8. Claim Authorization

Customers should contact PrimeShield before beginning a repair that they believe may be covered. PrimeShield may require information about the vehicle, diagnosis, repair facility, maintenance history, and estimated repair cost before authorizing a covered repair.

Repairs performed without required authorization may not qualify for reimbursement or payment under the applicable plan.

9. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for properly maintaining and operating their vehicle and for providing accurate information when purchasing a plan or submitting a claim.

Customers may be required to provide reasonable documentation, including repair estimates, diagnostic information, maintenance records, or other information necessary to evaluate a claim.

10. Repair Facilities

PrimeShield may require repairs to be performed at a repair facility that meets applicable requirements or may require prior authorization before work begins. The specific claim and repair process will depend on the customer's plan and the circumstances of the repair.

11. Plan Duration & Automatic Renewal

Plans are billed monthly and automatically renew unless canceled in accordance with the applicable cancellation terms.

Continued coverage requires continued payment and compliance with the terms of the selected plan.

12. Cancellation & Refunds

Customers may request cancellation of their plan by contacting PrimeShield Support. Cancellation and any applicable refund will be handled according to the customer's contract, applicable plan terms, and applicable law.

A cancellation does not automatically create a right to a full refund, particularly where coverage has already been provided or a claim has been paid or approved, subject to applicable law and the customer's contract.

13. Misuse & Fraud

Fraudulent claims, intentionally providing false information, deliberately causing vehicle damage, or other misuse of the service may result in denial of a claim, suspension, or termination of the account, subject to the customer's contract and applicable law.

14. Accuracy of Customer Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information regarding their identity, vehicle, mileage, ownership, vehicle condition, and other information requested during enrollment or the claims process.

Materially inaccurate or fraudulent information may affect eligibility for coverage or a claim.

15. No Guarantee of Claim Approval

Purchasing a PrimeShield plan does not guarantee that every repair or mechanical issue will be covered. Coverage is determined according to the specific plan purchased and its applicable terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations.

16. Plan Pricing

PrimeShield plans are offered at the prices displayed at the time of purchase. Current plan pricing may include Basic, Standard, and Premium options. The customer's applicable price and selected plan will be reflected in the customer's enrollment and account records.

17. Changes to Plans or Terms

PrimeShield may update its plans, services, or terms when permitted by applicable law. Any changes affecting an existing customer's contract will be handled according to the customer's applicable agreement and applicable legal requirements.

18. Applicable Contract

These website terms provide general information about PrimeShield vehicle protection plans. The customer's actual coverage is governed by the applicable vehicle protection/service contract issued at the time of enrollment, together with any applicable disclosures, limitations, and state-specific requirements.

19. Important Coverage Notice

PrimeShield vehicle protection plans are intended to provide protection for eligible covered repairs according to the applicable contract. Coverage is not unlimited, and exclusions, limitations, waiting periods, eligibility requirements, and claim procedures may apply.

If you have questions about your coverage, account, payment, plan, cancellation, or claim, please contact our support team.
